FAQ

Good to know.

Still stuck? Message any branch and we will help you out.

Is the first lesson really free?

Yes. Book a trial and play with a teacher. They will recommend a program after hearing you play.

Do I need experience?

No. Complete beginners are welcome, and your teacher builds the lessons around where you are.

How do lessons work?

Lessons are one-on-one, one hour each, usually twice a week. Your teacher plans each session around songs you love.

Which instruments can I learn?

Vocals, violin, guitar, bass, drums, piano, and ukulele.

Can students join talent shows?

Yes. Every student can join our talent shows and play for a real audience.
