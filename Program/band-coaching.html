BAND COACHING

Get your band stage-ready with a working band musician.

For students who want to experience the chemistry of playing in a live band. Mentored by working gigging musicians, you learn how to lock in with a bassist and drummer, blend vocals, manage stage volume, and move through setlists without missing a beat.

IDEAL FOR

Solo students wanting a band experience, or pre-formed local bands needing professional arrangement and live-performance direction.

PROGRAM DETAILS
Sessions
2 band sessions
Band jams
4 hours of weekend band jams
Led by
A working band musician
Validity
Use within 1 year
OBJECTIVES
  • Stage chemistry and ensemble timing
  • Vocal blending and stage volume control
  • Dynamic setlist execution under a gigging musician
